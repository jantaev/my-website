About me

I'm a frontend developer with a background in web design and UI/UX. I started my career in web design in 2016 and transitioned into frontend development in 2020.

I currently work at Urbanlinx Media, contributing to HotNewHipHop, where I help build and maintain modern web applications. My work includes developing reusable UI components, migrating legacy PHP-based pages to modern frameworks, improving performance and technical SEO, and working with technologies such as React, Next.js, Astro, and TypeScript.

I'm interested in building better web experiences, exploring modern development workflows, and learning how AI tools and agents can improve the way we design, build, and maintain software.
